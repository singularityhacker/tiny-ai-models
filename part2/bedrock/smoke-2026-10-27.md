# Bedrock connectivity smoke — 2026-10-27 UTC

Outcome: managed ingestion and retrieval succeeded. Model generation blocked

not MMLU/HellaSwag evaluation or evidence of model quality.

## Scope and results

Region: us-west-2. Five deliberately selected Simple Wikipedia articles from
`20220301.simple`: Water, Gravity, Photosynthesis, Volcano, Moon. Total uploaded
text: 37,094 bytes. Private S3 bucket with public access blocked, SSE-S3 encryption
and one-day expiry as a backup. Dedicated Bedrock service role limited to reading
that bucket, with account and Knowledge Base ARN trust conditions.

Managed KB used managed embeddings. Ingestion completed in about two minutes:
five documents scanned/indexed, zero failed, zero skipped.

Three hybrid Retrieve calls used `managedSearchConfiguration`, reranking NONE,
and three results each. All three requests succeeded.

| Probe | Observation |
| --- | --- |
| Gas released during photosynthesis | Retrieved Photosynthesis passages containing oxygen |
| Cause of ocean tides | Retrieved unrelated Water passages; selected Moon/Gravity articles did not provide a direct explanation of ocean tides |
| Molten rock reaching the surface | Retrieved Volcano passages describing lava |

The probes were manually chosen, not a random evaluation sample. Relevance scores
are ranking signals, not confidence or factual-accuracy scores. The corpus itself
contains outdated/inaccurate statements and missing values from source formatting;
retrieving a passage does not establish that its statements are true. A frozen
corpus quality audit is needed before interpreting benchmark results.
